Independent monitoring & collation of Nigerian election results — powered by citizens, not government.

NaijaPVT is a private parallel vote tabulation platform. We deploy multiple trained representatives at each sampled polling unit. They report results independently and live, upload photographic evidence, and our system cross-checks them against each other before collating — automatically.

How it works

Recruit & deploy reps

We recruit and vet trained representatives — starting with a 1% sample of Nigeria's polling units, stratified across states, LGAs and wards — and assign 3 reps to each polling unit, so every result is witnessed and reported independently three times.

Live result submission

Each rep types the figures they witnessed (votes per party, accredited voters) and uploads a photograph of the posted result sheet (Form EC8A). Each rep may report only once per polling unit.

Rep cross-checking

The system automatically compares the 3 reps' figures for the same polling unit. If all three agree → the result is locked as verified. If anyone's figures differ → the PU is flagged for your collation team to review the photos and resolve.

Automatic collation & comparison

Verified figures roll up automatically into live totals by ward, LGA and state. Our collation is then compared against officially announced results — any mismatch is flagged instantly for investigation.

Why multiple reps per polling unit?

Three independent reports let the collation team spot differences and review the posted result sheet. Matching reports provide a cross-check; they do not by themselves prove that a result is official or correct.

Platform safeguards

• 3 reps per polling unit, independent reports compared automatically
• One submission per rep — locked after upload
• Mandatory photo evidence of the posted result sheet
• Timestamped submissions
• Reps vetted and trained before deployment
• Private platform — not affiliated with INEC or any government body
