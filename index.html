How it works

Recruit & deploy reps

The planned sampling approach starts with a 1% stratified sample of Nigeria's polling units. Each selected unit is assigned 3 trained representatives. The sample size and weights must be validated before results can represent broader totals.

Independent result reports

Once account access is connected, each assigned rep will enter the figures they witnessed and upload a photograph of the posted result sheet (Form EC8A). Each rep may report only once per polling unit.

Rep cross-checking

The planned system compares the 3 reports for each polling unit. Matching figures can qualify for collation; differences are flagged for the collation team to review against the submitted evidence.

Collation & comparison

After the shared database and review process are in place, verified figures can roll up by ward, LGA and state. Comparison with officially announced results requires a validated sample frame and statistical weights.

Why multiple reps per polling unit?

Three independent reports let the collation team spot differences and review the posted result sheet. Matching reports provide a cross-check; they do not by themselves prove that a result is official or correct.

Platform safeguards

• 3 reps per polling unit, independent reports compared automatically
• One submission per rep — locked after upload
• Mandatory photo evidence of the posted result sheet
• Timestamped submissions
• Reps vetted and trained before deployment
• Private platform — not affiliated with INEC or any government body
